# Planning layer (plans, targets, Plan P&L)

Where: `/planning` (plans list) and `/planning/<planId>` (editor). Phases A and B built 2026-10-01. Later phases at the bottom.

## How it works
A **plan** is one version for one client and year. One plan per client and year is the **Default**; Home and the reports compare against it (`useDashboardData` picks the default plan whose year matches the range). The strategist types **monthly inputs**; a deterministic function (`lib/planning/generate.ts`) turns them into **daily rows** in `plan_targets`, which Home and the reports already read. No statistics in A and B.

Monthly inputs (`plan_months.inputs`, jsonb, type `PlanMonthInputs`): spend, aMER, returning revenue, platform % split, brand / non-acquisition % per platform, rates (discount, shipping, tax, refund, COGS, other variable costs, new and returning AOV), fixed costs (SG&A, only in the Plan P&L), optional fixed daily values.

Daily generation per month:
- Weekday weight = 1 + that weekday's offset x strength. Spend and returning revenue are spread over the days by these weights; a day with a fixed value keeps it and the other days absorb the difference.
- New revenue = acquisition spend x aMER (aMER keeps the dashboard definition: brand and Meta returning excluded when the client settings say so), so aMER is constant on free days (same as Statlas).
- Order revenue = new + returning. With b = gross - discounts: shipping = s*b, tax = t*b, refunds = r*b, order revenue = b*(1+s+t). Net sales = b - refunds.
- **CM = Net Sales + Shipping - COGS - Ad spend - Cost of delivery** (COGS = % of net sales, cost of delivery = % of net sales + shipping plus per-order cost). Identical to `cmParts`; a test fills a month from actuals and checks it reproduces the real CM within 1%.
- Platform split: fixed per month. Google alt = Brand, other platforms alt = non-acquisition (`*_returning` channels).

## Weekday pattern (from Shopify)
`dowFromHistory` (`lib/planning/history.ts`): last 12 months of order revenue, per weekday drop Black Friday week and days more than 2 standard deviations from that weekday's mean, average, offset = weekday average / mean of the 7 averages - 1 (offsets sum to ~0). Needs 8 weeks of data. Stored on the plan (`seasonality`), editable with sliders, "Recalculate", "Flat". Verified against Statlas: its offsets also sum to zero.

## Filling a month
"From last year" (same month, optional growth % on spend and returning), "From last 3 months", "From actuals", "Blank". Copies spend split, aMER, rates, AOVs and fixed costs from real data (`inputsFromFacts`). "Actualize elapsed months" fills every elapsed month from actuals. Locked months are never changed.

## Editor steps (restructured 2026-10-02 to the Statlas order)
Tabs: **Overview** (monthly grid, fills, locks) - **Models** (Info with icons, New Customer, Returning, Event) - **Calendar** - **Spend** - **Details** - **Input** - **P&L**. The text below describes the original four steps; Spend now also has last-year comparison, charts and event chips, Details is a dropdown of lines (below), Input shows events and all P&L lines with CSV download.
1 Monthly plan (year grid) - 2 Spend & days (month detail, platform sliders with locks, weekday pattern, daily table with fixed days) - 3 Returning revenue (per month vs last year; this is the override column until the cohort model exists) - 4 Daily input (final rows, CSV) - 5 Plan P&L (draft). Nothing changes until **Save plan**. Plan settings (gear on the card): name, description, make default, lock and status (in progress / final) per month, change log, delete.

## Plan P&L and Forecast
`lib/reports/plan-pnl.ts`: plan days are converted to actual-shaped days so `buildPnl` renders actual, plan and forecast with the same lines. `/reporting/pnl` Compare to: **A** actual, **P** plan (all months), **F** actuals up to the last data day plus plan for the rest. Plan columns use the plan's own COGS and cost of delivery; SG&A comes from the plan (client setting for elapsed months in F). Paid vs Organic and the ad-performance blocks are not planned (hidden / replaced in P and F).

## Models (built 2026-10-02, follows the Statlas screenshots in `Statlas Original UX/Forecasting and Calendar Layer/`)
Editor tab **Models** (first tab): Info cards, New Customer Model, Returning Customer Model (Event Model comes with the Calendar). Models only use the client's own history (baseline); the strategist bets against them. **Actualizing is built into both models.**
- **New Customer Model** (`lib/planning/new-customer.ts`, UI `components/planning/models/NewCustomerModel.tsx`), **reworked 2026-10-02 to follow Jack's aMER_ModelV2 C code** (read and run; test `amer-model-v2.test.ts` reproduces its table from `amer-model-v2.fixture.json`): per day, aMER (new revenue / spend that counts toward aMER) is weekday-adjusted, BFCM week removed, then outliers dropped with **Jack's spend-bucket ±1σ filter** (IQR optional; when actualizing, recent and older days are filtered separately), then **straight-line OLS of adjusted revenue (spend × aMER) on daily spend**: `rev = a + b·spend`, aMER = b + a/spend, `a` = implied revenue at $0 spend, R² on revenue. "aMER line" and "Flattening curve" and "Best fit" remain as options. Uses **all history since store inception** (`usePlanHistory` loads from 2010). Shown monthly (daily x days). Spend table has Max CM, **Max 60-day CM**, **Max 1-year CM** (replaces Statlas's "Lifetime"), **Max Revenue at break-even** (CM >= 0 on the first order, as Jack's "Max nRev B/E"); rows above the highest observed daily spend are greyed as extrapolated. **Monthly seasonality of spending power** (`monthlyAmerIndex`): per calendar month, adjusted aMER / curve over past years (needs 2 years, shrunk toward 1), applied to each month's table and curve. Chart colours: grey history, orange same month last year, blue this month this year, green Max points, red selected. **Create forecast** button (rule: Max CM default, Max 60-day, Max 1-year, Max revenue B/E, or last year + growth %) sets every unlocked month and saves. CM = new revenue x (1 - cost %) - spend. LTV = AOV x multiplier; multipliers "Recommended" from cohorts (`cohortMultipliers`) or manual. AOV and cost % default to **last 30 days**, or 90/365 days or manual. "% over model" defaults to the 90th percentile of days vs the line. Min CM limits constrain the Max markers. **Actualize with recent data**: days since a chosen date count x2-12 in a weighted fit, with before/after preview. Save Model stores the model and writes spend (divided by the acquisition share) and aMER into the plan months (locked months skipped); Save plan then writes daily targets. **Guardrail beyond history (2026-10-02):** the fitted line is only trusted up to the 95th-percentile daily spend; past it the marginal return per extra $ is the marginal at that edge times (edge/spend)^gamma (Mild 0.5, Standard 1, Strict 2; default Standard, setting `beyondHistory`), so 10x the spend brings a tenth of the edge return and Max CM gets an interior optimum a little beyond history. Table runs to 3x history, rows past it are italic/grey, a red note appears when the chosen spend is beyond it. Chart is aMER vs spend only; last year's month and this month so far are single clickable points, Max markers have their own colours and are clickable, dashed line = highest spend seen. Closed months show actuals. Jack's code quirks (not copied): config hardcoded, CSV spend parser breaks under $1,000 or over $1M, flat 30-day month.
- **Returning Customer Model** (`lib/planning/returning.ts`, UI `ReturningModel.tsx`): cohorts by acquisition month with repeat revenue in 30-day periods (migration 0007 tables `cohort_stats`, `cohort_periods`, function `rebuild_cohorts`, built from ALL stored orders). Lift curve = revenue-weighted average of the latest N cohorts per age, decaying past the observed age. Forecast = existing cohorts' remaining revenue + **future cohorts (plan new revenue x same lifts)** + pre-history customers (fitted decay) x calendar-month index from past years. **Actualize**: calibration factor = last N months' cohort revenue / model prediction, applied forward. Output fills `returningModel` and `returningRevenue` of plan months; Details has a per-month **Override** (`returningOverride`) that beats the model until cleared.
- `plan_models` table (migration 0007) saves each model's settings/results per plan. Repository: `getCohortData`, `rebuildCohorts`, `getPlanModel`, `savePlanModel`.
- **Migration 0007 must be applied** (SQL in `supabase/migrations/0007_cohorts_and_models.sql`; run it in the Supabase SQL editor, then press "Build cohorts from orders"). Until then the Returning model shows a build button and saving models fails with a clear message.

## Calendar and Event Effect Model (built 2026-10-02)
Code: `lib/planning/events.ts` (types, Statlas event-type list, measurement, category stats, lifts, CSV parser, spike suggestions), `components/planning/CalendarStep.tsx`, `components/planning/models/EventModel.tsx`, `generateMonth(events)` in `generate.ts`. Migration `0008_marketing_calendar` (**written, NOT applied**): adds promo/product/audience/segment_size/notes to `marketing_events` and insert/update/delete policies. Event model settings are stored per plan in `plan_models` kind `event` (needs 0007).
- **Calendar tab:** month list by day, events as chips (colour by group), last year's events (364 days earlier, same weekday) greyed, add/edit/delete inline, **Import CSV** (template, header matching, DD/MM/YYYY or ISO), **Suggest events from data** (unlabelled revenue spikes >=1.4x ordinary level over 2 years, merged into runs). Initiative type is logged only.
- **Measuring:** each past event day vs the median of ordinary same-period days (+-28 days, event days and BFCM excluded, weekday-adjusted): spend lift, new revenue lift, returning lift, aMER lift. Category table per type: events, days, median, 90% range (5th-95th percentile), aMER impact, "few events" flag (<3).
- **Future events:** strength Conservative / Typical / Strong = lower quartile / median / upper quartile of the type's own days, pulled toward all-events when few days (`(n*own + 5*all)/(n+5)`); a type with no history gets half the all-events effect. Per-event override of the lifts, "compare" panel with the last 5 same-type events (tick, use average), turn event off. Overlap: the strongest offer/launch/holiday/BFCM wins; email/SMS/PR only count alone.
- **Applying:** event days get spend weight x spend lift and returning weight x returning lift; new revenue uses aMER x aMER lift re-centred on the spend-weighted month average, so **month totals stay what the models and strategist set**; other days are compressed. Master switch "Apply events to the plan days". Saving events marks every planned month for Save plan.
