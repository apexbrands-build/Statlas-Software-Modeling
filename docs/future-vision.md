# Future vision (parked ideas and direction, 2026-10-11)

Not scheduled. The build order for the channels work lives in its own specs (`docs/superpowers/specs/`, once written).

## Direction chosen (strategist, 2026-10-11)
Goal: roll Apex Analytics out to the 30+ clients. Build the day-to-day ecom foundation first: Shopify, ad channels, email, SMS, GA4. Cost-only channels and TikTok Shop come after.

## What most clients run
- Paid: Meta and Google Ads (built). Other ad platforms (TikTok, Pinterest, Bing, Snapchat, YouTube, AppLovin) have slots in `lib/channels.ts` and get a connector when a client needs one.
- Email: Klaviyo. SMS: Klaviyo SMS, Postscript or Omnisend. Analytics: GA4 on almost every client.

## Parked: TikTok Shop and cost-only channels (Tribe, creator and affiliate spend)
- TikTok Shop is managed in other tools. The software only needs its cost (and orders) so contribution margin and MER are not overstated. Without creator commissions, affiliate fees, seeding and Tribe-type spend the CM is wrong.
- Most smaller clients have small creator programs, so this waits until a client needs it.
- Likely shape: a "manual / other spend" channel (Statlas MER has "Manual Other Spend"): cost lines by category, typed or CSV-imported, counted in total spend and CM, kept out of aMER unless flagged. TikTok Shop orders as a sales channel that can be included or excluded from the marketing and forecast models.

## Creative intelligence layer (its own sub-project)
Wanted: visibility into creative inside the software, pulled from Meta at ad level, plus a flag when a winner starts spending significantly more week over week. Depth closer to Motion or Atria than to a basic report. Not a copy of Statlas Best Creatives.
- Reference (screenshot, 2026-10-11, example account had no data because its Meta was disconnected): ad list loaded on demand, user-picked metric chips (Spend, CM, Revenue, ROAS), store averages for the period as the benchmark, toggles for campaign name and ad title.
- Needs: an ad-level daily table (today we store campaigns only).
- Ideas: scaling-winner detector (spend up X% week over week with ROAS above the store average), fatigue signal, creative demand model (new ads needed per month from planned spend and the calendar), tags such as format, hook and angle later.
- Rule to settle before building: CLAUDE.md says never build Best Creatives or Global Creatives. This layer needs that rule amended.

## Build queue (inputs received 2026-10-12)
Specs now written in `docs/superpowers/specs/`: `2026-10-12-four-reports-design.md`, `2026-10-12-forecast-summary-design.md`, `2026-10-12-channel-mix-design.md`, `2026-10-12-markets-design.md`, `2026-10-12-email-sms-plan-and-trackers-design.md`. Still to spec: Creative Demand, Four Peaks / moments library. The notes below are the source material.
The Canon names five forecast models (`Agency/Training/CTC Canon/forecasting.md`): Spending Power, Retention and Event Effect are built; **Creative Demand and MMM are the two missing**, and they are what the strategist asked about.
- **Forecast summary view (replaces the Notepad's forecast tab).** Month by month and year over year: spend, aMER, new revenue, returning revenue, total revenue, contribution profit, and opex down to EBITDA. Shows spend growth against efficiency change per month, to see where a plan asks for a lot of spend at the same efficiency, and compares scenarios. Needs the Notepad's forecast, scenario and cohort tabs, which only exist in the full sheet (the CSV in `Agency/Training/` holds just the Google Ads ingest tab). Related: `docs/scenarios-and-diagnostic.md`.
- **History-based assumptions.** The Notepad pulls Shopify, Meta and Google monthly (bronze and silver tabs) and infers gross sales, discount and shipping percentages from history instead of last year's values. Our Details step already infers from last year and 90 days; the gap to close is read from the Notepad tabs.
- **Channel allocation (MMM and spend power).** From The Bridge (`Agency/Training/Copy of The Bridge.pdf`, text extracted; p.28 to 33): total budget comes from the spend/aMER model, channel split from the MMM, platform ROAS target = aMER target ÷ incrementality factor (built). Spend power = total spend needed to raise weighted CAC by $1, a measure of market elasticity; suggested spend follows it (Meta about 75%, Google about 25 to 30% in many months and as low as 10% in big months, Snapchat about 3%, TikTok unclear). The slide gives the definition and outputs, not the formula, and the chart is an image. Canon describes MMM as marginal ROI per channel (Bayesian, adstock and saturation), monthly. Today the allocation in our plan is fixed percentage sliders. Idea for a first version: a response curve per channel from `daily_channel_facts`, calibrated by the incrementality factors, allocating to equalise marginal return, with guardrails. Needs its own spec after the Statlas MMM behaviour is understood. Benchmarks on p.29 not yet in our defaults: YouTube 75%, conversion social (TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat) 30%, awareness and CTV 30%.
- **Creative Demand model.** Canon formula (`creative-strategy.md`): new ads needed = (target spend - current spend x carry rate) / expected spend per new ad, with carry rate about 70 to 75% and first-month spend per new ad about $192 to $397. Needs Meta ad-level daily spend (new table) for carry rate, tiers (whale, winner, testing, dust) and spend per new ad. The Bridge (p.39 to 48) frames it as Ultimate Brief (audiences, offers, angles, quantity of ads) feeding a monthly Concept Log tied to the spend plan. Goes with the creative intelligence layer above.
- **Four Peaks and model-breaking moments.** The Bridge (p.3 to 25): most calendars have two revenue peaks; the Four Peaks approach plans more. Each example records objective, result and "over model" percentage (for example +35% to +56%). Idea for the Calendar: a result field per past event showing actual against the model, so moments build a library. Examples themselves are training material.

## Asks recorded 2026-10-12 (designs awaiting the strategist's approval)
- **Built 2026-10-12: 90-day payback window in the New Customer (spend and aMER) model.** Today the model has 60-day and 1-year LTV points (`ltv60Multiplier`, `ltv365Multiplier`, `minCm60`, `minCm365`, "Max 60-day CM", "Max 1-year CM"). Add a third point: `ltv90Multiplier`, `minCm90`, "Max 90-day CM" as a spend-table marker and a Create forecast rule. No migration: 90-day repeat revenue is the first three 30-day periods already stored in `cohort_periods`; "Recommended" multiplier = the same 12 most recent mature cohorts rule as 60 and 365 days, mature meaning 90 days after the month ended. Models saved before this have no 90-day values and fall back to the recommended value.
- **Built 2026-10-12: retention spend toggle for aMER** (the switch now covers every platform's returning spend). The setting already exists per client in Store Settings > aMER/CAC Calculation ("Exclude Search Brand Spend" and "Exclude Social Non-Acquisition Spend", `excludeBrandFromAmer`, `excludeMetaReturningFromAmer`): aMER and the new-customer model leave that spend out while total spend and MER keep it, and the plan's daily spend still adds both. Proposed: show the same two switches inside the New Customer model so they can be changed where the model is read (same client setting, so Home, reports and the model always agree), and make the "social non-acquisition" switch cover every platform's returning channel (today `amerSpend` and plan generation only remove Meta returning, although the label says social non-acquisition). That second part changes aMER for any client with Pinterest, TikTok or other returning spend, so it needs the strategist's yes.
- **Subscription layer for returning revenue (draft understanding, needs a spec).** Store Settings > Sales channels lets the strategist mark which channels count as DTC; subscription apps (Skio, Recharge and similar) appear there as channels, so add a "Subscription" tag per channel to split returning revenue into subscription rebills and other returning revenue in history. Forecast returning revenue = subscription rebills + other returning (cohort model fed only non-subscription orders). Pull upcoming rebills (date, amount, status per subscription) from the subscription app's API into a daily snapshot, put the expected revenue on the exact rebill days (scaled by the share of scheduled rebills that historically happened), and beyond the API's horizon project each subscription by its interval and the observed cancel and skip rate. Plan targets gain a subscription revenue line so daily targets spike on rebill days; Home gets a Subscriptions section. The app connector fits the connector foundation (kind subscriptions, API key per client, Skio first). Apps wanted: Skio, Recharge, Loop, Smartrr and Seal. Shopify orders only show rebills that already happened, so the forward schedule needs each app's API (to my knowledge Shopify's own subscription contract data is readable only by the app that created the contract; verify). Fallback for an app without API access: infer the next rebill date of each subscriber from their past subscription order cadence. To verify before build: each app's API fields and limits, and how subscription orders appear in Shopify (channel name or source) for the history split.
