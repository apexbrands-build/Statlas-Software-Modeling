# Shopify screencast: Apex Analytics (about 2.5 minutes, English)

Rewritten 2026-11-23 for the install-opens-the-store flow (the 2026-10-15 script showed the Apex login and the manual linking step, which a reviewer cannot do). No Markets, no country code.

## Before you record

- Tab 1: Shopify admin of a development store with a few orders, including at least one placed today. Tab 2: empty.
- Have Shopify admin → Analytics ready in tab 1 to compare numbers.
- Browser zoom 110%, other tabs and notifications closed. One dry run first. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** and paste the link in the submission.

## The steps

### 1. Install (0:00)
**Show:** the install link, Shopify's permission screen. Keep the data lines on screen while you speak.
**Say:** "This is Apex Analytics, a free, read-only analytics app. Shopify lists what it can view: orders, customers, products and inventory. It reads order amounts, dates and refunds, a numeric customer ID to tell new from returning customers, and product costs. No names, emails, phones or addresses, and it never writes to the store."

### 2. The store page opens (0:30)
**Show:** click Install, land on the store page. Wait while "Loading your order history" counts up and the numbers fill in.
**Say:** "Installing is all a merchant does. The app connects the store and loads its orders by itself, then shows the store's sales."

### 3. The numbers (0:55)
**Show:** the Total sales breakdown for the last 30 days, then New and returning customers, then the By day table.
**Say:** "Gross sales, discounts, returns, net sales, shipping, taxes and total sales, in the store's currency and time zone, then new versus returning customers and every day."

### 4. Compare with Shopify (1:20)
**Show:** tab 1, Shopify Analytics with the same 30 days: point at Total sales and Orders. Back to the app.
**Say:** "Shopify Analytics for the same dates shows the same total sales and orders."

### 5. A new order shows up (1:40)
**Show:** in Shopify, create an order (or a draft order marked paid). Then Apps → Apex Analytics: the page says "Checking Shopify for new orders…", then the new order is in Today.
**Say:** "Every time the app opens it checks Shopify for new and changed orders, so an order placed a minute ago is already here."

### 6. Privacy (2:05)
**Show:** https://apex-data-beta.vercel.app/privacy
**Say:** "Our privacy policy states exactly what we read. We handle Shopify's privacy webhooks and erase a store's data on request."

### 7. Uninstall (2:20)
**Show:** Shopify admin → Settings → Apps → Apex Analytics → Uninstall.
**Say:** "Uninstalling stops access immediately. Thank you."
